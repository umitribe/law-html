2026-20. Southwood Construction Contract and Limited Sovereign Immunity Waiver

Law not yet effective

This document is not yet codified because it is not yet effective. It will become effective on September 28, 2026.

A Resolution authorizing a commercial construction contract with Southwood Building Systems, Inc. and providing a limited waiver of sovereign immunity in connection with the contract.

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribe (the "Tribe") is an Indian Tribe that has existed from time immemorial and is federally recognized on a government-to-government basis by the United States of America; and,

WHEREAS, the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe (the "Constitution") was adopted to ensure the Tribe's well-being by fostering self-sufficiency, promoting sustainable development, and securing the economic future of the Tribe and its citizens; and,

WHEREAS, Article VII, Section 4(a) of the Constitution vests legislative power in the Council of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe (the "Council") to enact laws and budget resolutions that support these interests; and,

WHEREAS, the Tribe is planning certain construction projects and is negotiating a commercial contract with Southwood Building Systems, Inc., to construct a retail commercial building on the property identified as Parcel ID No. 29-56, located near the intersection of Cuz Way and King William Road; and

WHEREAS, as a condition of entering into such commercial contract, Southwood Building Systems, Inc. is seeking a limited waiver of the Tribe's sovereign immunity from suit; and

WHEREAS, Article XIII, Section 1 of the Constitution provides that "[o]nly the Council and Chief may authorize a waiver of Sovereign Immunity by law enacted in accordance with the Legislative Process. Any authorization by the Council to waive Sovereign Immunity shall be specific, for a limited scope and duration, and in writing."

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Chief of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, or their designee, is hereby authorized to negotiate, execute and deliver any and all agreements, instruments, certifications, and documents necessary or convenient to enter into a commercial contract with Southwood Building Systems, Inc.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Tribe hereby provides a limited waiver of its sovereign immunity and the sovereign immunity of its wholly owned limited liability company, UMT Heritage, LLC, for matters arising from or relating to: (i) a commercial contract between the Tribe or arm of the Tribe and Southwood Building Systems, Inc., regarding the construction of a retail commercial building on the property identified as Parcel ID No. 29-56, located near the intersection of Cuz Way and King William Road, and any and all ancillary documents and transactions in connection with such contract; and (ii) enforcement of such contract and ancillary documents and transactions and the rights, duties, and obligations therein, including but not limited to the payment of moneys owed, up to the maximum amount permitted pursuant to the commercial contract and the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia, and any other applicable relief permitted under the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia, in the courts of any city or county in the Commonwealth of Virginia and the federal courts of the Eastern District of Virginia.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Tribe agrees that all documents, occurrences, or transactions relating to or evidencing the commercial contract shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia and consents to the jurisdiction of the courts of any city or county in the Commonwealth of Virginia and the federal courts of the Eastern District of Virginia.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Tribe agrees that this waiver of sovereign immunity with regards to the commercial contract herein is binding, cannot be revoked, and extends in duration until the performance of all obligations by all parties therein has been completed.

BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that the Tribe authorizes the Chief of the Tribe to act as the authorized representative of the Tribal Government and the Tribe relating to the commercial contract and associated documents and/or transactions, including granting authority to sign any and all documents relating to the commercial contract and the waiver of sovereign immunity for the Tribe on behalf of the Tribe. This authority is granted to the Chief or their designee solely for the commercial contract between the Tribe or an arm of the Tribe and Southwood Building Systems, Inc. and all documents executed or to be executed in connection therewith.

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing Resolution was duly voted upon by the Council on the 26th day of September, 2026, at a General Meeting with a vote of 6 in favor and 0 opposed, 0 abstaining, and 1 absent, pursuant to the authority vested in the Council by the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.
