2026-21. Authorization of Aylett Health Properties

A Resolution to authorize the Chief, or their designee, to submit a Fee-to-Trust Application for the Aylett Health properties, consisting of five parcels of fee land identified as Parcel IDs 22-38, 22-34J, 22-34D, 22-34C, and 22-34B located in King William County.

At a duly called General Meeting of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council held this 24th day of October, 2026, the following Resolution and Law were adopted.

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribe ("Tribe") is an Indian Tribe that has existed from time immemorial and is federally recognized on a government-to-government basis by the United States of America under Section 303(a)(1) of the Thomasina E. Jordan Indian Tribes of Virginia Federal Recognition Act of 2017, Public Law No. 115-121 (January 29, 2018) ("Recognition Act"); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Article VII of the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe ("Constitution"), the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council ("Council") is vested with the legislative power of the Tribe and has the authority to enact laws pursuant to Article VII, Section 4(a) of the Constitution; and

WHEREAS, Section 306(a)(2) of the Recognition Act provides that the Secretary of the Department of the Interior ("Interior") may take land held in fee by the Tribe within King William County, Caroline County, Hanover County, King and Queen County, and New Kent County, Virginia into trust; and

WHEREAS, Section 5 of the Indian Reorganization Act, 25 U.S.C. § 5108, authorizes the Secretary of the Interior to acquire land in trust for the benefit of an Indian tribe; and

WHEREAS, the Tribe established its Reservation through the fee-to-trust process on April 14, 2022, and, pursuant to the Trust Land Acquisition Statute of 2024 (Title 42, Chapter 3), has established its authority and duty to pursue trust status for additional Tribal lands; and

WHEREAS, the Tribe holds in fee the following five parcels of land, which are located in King William County, Virginia, are more particularly described in the deeds, plats, and other land records submitted with the Tribe's fee-to-trust application, and have adequate ingress, egress, and access to services (collectively, the "Properties"):

  • Parcel 22-34B: 7864 Richmond-Tappahannock Highway, Aylett, VA 23009
  • Parcel 22-34C: 7840 Richmond-Tappahannock Highway, Aylett, VA 23009
  • Parcel 22-34D: 7772 Richmond-Tappahannock Highway, Aylett, VA 23009
  • Parcel 22-38: 8014 Richmond-Tappahannock Highway, Aylett, VA 23009
  • Parcel 22-34J: No assigned street address; and

WHEREAS, Section 306(c) of the Recognition Act provides that lands acquired in trust shall, upon request of the Tribe, be considered part of the Tribe's Reservation, and Section 7 of the Indian Reorganization Act, 25 U.S.C. § 5110, authorizes the Secretary of the Interior to add lands to an existing reservation; and

WHEREAS, the Tribe intends to continue using the Properties in support of the Tribe's health, governmental, economic development, leasing, permitting, and related purposes, and believes that acquiring the Properties in trust will further Tribal self-determination and protect the health, welfare, and sovereignty of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe; and

WHEREAS, the Tribe is applying to the Bureau of Indian Affairs to transfer fee simple title to the Properties to the United States in trust for the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, and authorization is required for Tribal officials to execute the documents necessary to complete the fee-to-trust application.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Upper Mattaponi Tribe hereby requests that the United States Department of the Interior, Bureau of Indian Affairs acquire the Properties in trust for the Upper Mattaponi Tribe pursuant to Section 306(a)(2) of the Recognition Act, Section 5 of the Indian Reorganization Act, 25 U.S.C. § 5108, and 25 C.F.R. part 151; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Upper Mattaponi Tribe hereby authorizes the Chief of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, or their designee, to execute all documents relevant to the Tribe's fee-to-trust application for the Properties; and

BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that the Upper Mattaponi Tribe hereby requests that, upon acquisition of the Properties in trust for the benefit of the Tribe, the Secretary of the Interior proclaim said lands as part of the Upper Mattaponi Reservation pursuant to Section 306(c) of the Recognition Act and Section 7 of the Indian Reorganization Act, 25 U.S.C. § 5110.

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing Resolution was duly voted upon by the Council on the 24th day of October, 2026, at a General Meeting with a vote of 6 in favor and 0 opposed, 0 abstaining, and 1 absent, pursuant to the authority vested in the Council by the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.
