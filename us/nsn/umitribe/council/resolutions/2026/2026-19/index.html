2026-19. Approving the Upper Mattaponi Tribe's FY 2027-2031 Transportation Priority List

A Resolution to establish the Upper Mattaponi Tribe's five-year (FY 2027-2031) Transportation Priority List and authorize the Chief or their designee to execute necessary contracts and agreements to advance key transportation planning initiatives, including Long-Range Transportation Plan development, National Tribal Transportation Facility Inventory creation, and Transportation Safety Plan development. The resolution commits available Tribal Transportation Program and Special Appropriation funding to support these foundational planning activities as required under federal transportation regulations.

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council ("Council"), pursuant to the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, possesses legislative authority to establish policy and priorities on behalf of the Tribe; and

WHEREAS, the Bureau of Indian Affairs, Eastern Regional Office, Division of Transportation has received Special Appropriation funds and Tribal Transportation Program funds as defined in 23 U.S.C. 202; and

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council desires to utilize the current Special Appropriation funds made available, all Transportation Program funds, and any future Tribal Transportation Program and Special Appropriation funding that will be made available to the Upper Mattaponi Tribe; and

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council hereby identifies and adopts the following Transportation Priority List of roads and transportation projects in accordance with 25 CFR 170.402 for FY 2027-FY 2031, to be funded, in no exact order, using future and current Tribal Transportation Program funding, Special Appropriation funding and any other funding that may become and be made available to the Upper Mattaponi Tribe:

1. Development and implementation of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe's Long-Range Transportation Plan (LRTP);

2. Development and maintenance of the National Tribal Transportation Facility Inventory (NTTFI);

3. Development and implementation of a Tribal Transportation Safety Plan;

4. All allowable Transportation Planning activities as defined in Appendix A to subpart D as defined in 25 CFR 170.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council hereby authorizes the Chief of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe or their designee to sign all documents necessary to enter into or modify a PL 93-638 contract, address planning, design and construction; to address road maintenance, safety plans and management; and to amend the Tribal Transportation Improvement Plan, Long Range Transportation Plan, Safety Plan, Tribal Priority List and the National Tribal Transportation Facility Inventory to add, update, amend, or delete roads and routes.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Chief and Tribal Council do hereby approve, affirm, adopt and submit to the Bureau of Indian Affairs, Eastern Regional Office, Division of Transportation its FY 2027-2031 Priority List.

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing Resolution was duly voted upon by the Council on the 26th day of September, 2026, at a General Meeting with a vote of 6 in favor and 0 opposed, 0 abstaining, and 1 absent, pursuant to the authority vested in the Council by the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.
